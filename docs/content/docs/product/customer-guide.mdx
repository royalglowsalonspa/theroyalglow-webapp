# Customer Guide

<Info>
  **Welcome!** This guide walks you through finding Royal Glow, booking an
  appointment, and making the most of memberships, loyalty gems, and offers. No
  technical knowledge needed — just follow along.
</Info>

This is your guide to using Royal Glow online. It walks you through finding the
salon, booking an appointment, understanding your bookings, and making the most
of memberships, loyalty gems, and offers.

## Discovering the salon

You can find Royal Glow in a few ways, and every one of them leads to the same
simple booking experience:

- The website at **theroyalglow.in**
- A **Google Maps / Google Business** listing (4.9★, 86 reviews)
- A **QR code poster** inside the salon
- An **Instagram or Facebook ad**

On the website you can browse services and prices, read about the team, see
current offers, and start a booking — all before signing in. You only need to
sign in when you're ready to actually book.

## Signing in

Royal Glow uses **Google sign-in** only — there's no separate password to
remember.

<Steps>
<Step title="Click &quot;Sign in with Google&quot;">
Start the sign-in from the homepage or header.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose your Google account">
Pick the account you'd like to use.
</Step>
<Step title="First time? Complete a quick setup">
If it's your **first time**, you'll be taken to a quick one-time setup
(onboarding) to complete your profile.
</Step>
</Steps>

### First-time setup (onboarding)

The first time you sign in, we collect a few details so we can serve you well:

- **Name** and **email** (filled in from Google — email can't be changed)
- **Phone number**
- **Date of birth** and **gender**
- **Consent choices:**
  - Agreeing to the Privacy Policy (**required** — your data is stored securely on Indian servers and never sold)
  - Allowing anonymous usage analytics (**optional**)
  - Receiving offers and updates by email and notifications (**optional**)

Once you submit, you're taken back to the homepage and you're ready to book.

<Info>
  Started a booking before signing in? Don't worry — we remember what you were
  doing. After you sign in, the booking dialog reopens right where you left off.
</Info>

## Booking an appointment

### How to open the booking dialog

Booking happens in a pop-up dialog **right on the homepage** — you're never sent
to a confusing separate page. You can open it by:

- Tapping the **Book Now** button on the homepage or in the header
- Tapping **Book This** on a service card or an offer
- Following a direct link such as `theroyalglow.in/?book=1` (these "deep links"
  open the booking dialog automatically — they're what the Google Maps button
  and the in-salon QR poster use)

### The 4 steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Date & time">
Your name and email are already filled in. Pick a date (only future dates;
closed days are greyed out) and choose an available time slot. Fully booked slots
are greyed out.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a category">
Switch between **Salon** and **SPA**, then pick one or more categories. One
booking is either Salon *or* SPA, not both — if you want both, make two bookings.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose services">
Pick the specific services you want. A running total shows at the bottom (for
example, "3 services · ₹3,500.00"). Some spa services let you choose a 60-minute
or 90-minute version on the same card.
</Step>
<Step title="Summary">
You'll see **"Booking Submitted!"** with your booking number, date and time, the
services, the total (including 18% GST), and a note that payment is at the salon.
You'll also see your gems balance.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Your booking starts as **Pending**, not Confirmed. That's normal — see the
  next section for what it means.
</Info>

### What each booking status means

A booking moves through clear stages. Here's what each one means for you:

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Pending">
Submitted. Our team will review it shortly. You can still edit, reschedule, or cancel.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Confirmed">
The salon has accepted your booking and assigned a staff member.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Rejected">
The salon couldn't take this booking (you'll see a reason, e.g. no staff free at that time). You can book again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="In progress">
You're at the salon and the service is underway.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Completed">
The service is done, your invoice is generated, and you've earned gems.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Cancelled">
The booking was cancelled (by you or the salon).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

You don't choose your staff member during booking — the front desk assigns the
right person. You can add a note (for example, "prefer Anjali for the haircut")
and they'll do their best to honour it.

## Managing your bookings

Go to **My Bookings** to see everything in one place, split into **Upcoming**
and **Past**. The actions available depend on the booking's status:

| Status | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Edit services, reschedule, or cancel |
| Confirmed | Reschedule or cancel |
| Completed | View your invoice or book again |
| Rejected | See the reason or book again |
| Cancelled | Book again |

### Rescheduling

- You can reschedule a booking **up to 2 times**.
- Each reschedule must be at least **1 hour** before the appointment.
- Need to change it a third time? Cancel and make a fresh booking instead.

### Cancelling

- Cancelling **more than 4 hours** before your appointment is completely free.
- Cancelling **within 4 hours** is still free, but it's noted on your account
  and the team may call to check in. There is **never a cancellation fee**.

<Warning>
  **Please show up for confirmed bookings.** Missing an appointment without
  cancelling is a "no-show." A few of these add a note to your account; many
  no-shows mean future bookings need manager approval. See the
  [no-show policy](/docs/product/admin-staff-guide#the-no-show-policy) for the
  details.
</Warning>

## SPA memberships

A SPA membership is a **prepaid block of spa hours** you buy once and use across
several visits. It's great value if you enjoy regular spa treatments.

| Tier | Hours | Price | Valid for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🥈 **Silver** | 8 hours | ₹10,000 | 90 days |
| 🥇 **Gold** | 15 hours | ₹15,000 | 90 days |
| 💎 **Platinum** | Custom | Custom | Custom |

How memberships work:

- Every tier gives you access to **all** spa services. The only limit is your
  remaining **hours**.
- Each visit deducts the time used from your balance — there's no payment at the
  counter for a membership session.
- Memberships are bought and set up by the salon team (visit or call the salon).
- You can have **one active membership** at a time.
- Hours **expire** at the end of the period and don't roll over, so book them in.
  We'll remind you 30 days, 7 days, and 1 day before expiry.

You can track your tier, hours remaining, expiry date, and full session history
on your **My SPA Membership** page (it appears once you have a membership).

<Info>
  Memberships don't earn gems — not on purchase and not on sessions. The value
  is in the discounted prepaid hours.
</Info>

## Loyalty gems

Gems are Royal Glow's reward for being a customer. Here's the simple version:

- **Earn:** you get **1 gem for every ₹100** you spend on services (rounded
  down). So a ₹1,300 visit earns 13 gems.
- **Redeem:** gems unlock specific services from the **Gems Catalogue** — they
  work like a reward you cash in, not a rupee discount off your bill.
- **Expiry:** gems last **365 days** from when you earn them, so use them within
  the year.

A few things to know:

- You don't earn gems on membership purchases or membership sessions.
- You can't use gems and an offer on the same booking — pick whichever saves you
  more.

Check your balance, browse what you can redeem, and see your full history on the
**Royal Gems** page.

## Offers and combos

The **Offers** page shows current promotions — for example "20% off facials" or
a combo bundle price. A few rules:

- You can use **one offer per day**.
- Offers are applied by the receptionist at checkout (not during online booking).
- Offers can't be combined with gems on the same visit.
- Offers apply to salon services, not spa memberships.

## Reviews

We collect reviews on **Google Maps** only — there's no in-app rating. After a
visit you'll get a friendly link by email and on your invoice. If you enjoyed
your experience, a Google review genuinely helps the salon.
