Offers are the salon's promotions: a percentage off some services, a flat rupee
discount, or a bundle of services at one combo price. Customers see them on the
`/offers` page, and the receptionist applies one at the counter when the
customer pays.

<Info>
  Offers are applied **at checkout by a receptionist** — they are not
  self-service during booking. This keeps control of discounts with staff and
  prevents stacking.
</Info>

## What it is

A flexible way to run promotions that drive visits — "20% off all Facials this
week", "₹500 off any Hair Treatment", or "Hair + Facial + Manicure = ₹2,999".
Each offer is authored in the CMS as a website card with an optional
**checkout rule**. A card without a rule is advertising only; a rule without a
card on the website (Show on website unticked) is a counter-only discount.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step title="Create">
An editor creates the offer in the CMS (`cms.theroyalglow.in`, Offers): the
card, its Valid from and Valid until dates, and the checkout rule's type,
value, services and terms. The CMS checks the rule on save: its services must
be active salon services.
</Step>
<Step title="Display">
While active and within its dates, an offer shown on the website appears on
the public `/offers` page with its terms.
</Step>
<Step title="Apply">
At checkout, the booking's panel lists the offers that apply to it, each with
what it saves. The receptionist picks one, or none, before taking payment.
</Step>
<Step title="End">
The offer stops applying after its Valid until date (IST). Untick Active in
the CMS to retire it earlier. Offers are never deleted.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Offer types

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Percentage">
**Type:** `percentage`

A percentage (1–100) off the booking's lines for the linked services, rounded
down to the paisa.

**Example:** "20% off all Facials".
</Tab>
<Tab title="Flat">
**Type:** `flat`

A fixed rupee amount off the linked lines, at most their total.

**Example:** "₹500 off any Hair Treatment".
</Tab>
<Tab title="Combo price">
**Type:** `combo_price`

The linked services together at one price. The booking must include every
linked service; the saving is their total less the combo price.

**Example:** "Hair + Facial + Manicure = ₹2,999".
</Tab>
</Tabs>

An offer that saves nothing on a booking, because the booking has none of its
services, isn't listed for it. The invoice shares the discount across the
linked lines only, so each line's tax follows its own share.

## Rules

<Warning>
  **One offer per customer per day** and **offers cannot combine with gems** — an
  offer and a [gems](/content/docs/features/loyalty-gems) redemption cannot both apply to
  the same booking. The daily limit counts the IST calendar day at every branch,
  and the database enforces it with a unique constraint on customer + date, so
  two checkouts at the same moment can't both use an offer.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Who applies an offer?">
A receptionist, at checkout — not the customer during booking. This keeps
control of discounts with staff and prevents stacking.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do offers apply to SPA services or memberships?">
No. Offers are **salon only**: spa bookings and membership sessions take no
offer. See [SPA Memberships](/content/docs/features/memberships).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What if the offer ends while the customer is at the counter?">
Checkout checks the offer again when it completes the booking. If the offer
was retired or its dates ended in between, completion is refused and nothing
is written; the panel refreshes its list so the receptionist can complete
without it or choose another.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does editing an offer change past invoices?">
No. The invoice keeps the offer's name, type and value as applied, and the
database refuses any later change to them.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Where are an offer's terms shown?">
Each offer carries its own terms (e.g. "Valid on weekdays only"), shown on the
card and in the checkout panel.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What happens to an offer if the booking is cancelled?">
Nothing is used up: an offer is recorded only when a booking is completed with
it, so the customer can still use one that day.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
